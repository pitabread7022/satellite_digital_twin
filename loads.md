# loads description

## Platform base load

15 W continuous (ADCS, OBC, COMMS idle, etc.)

## Payload imaging

+40 W during imaging (imaging only in sunlight), Nominal: 10 minutes of
imaging per orbit, entirely within sunlight

## Downlink (high-rate COMMS)

+30 W during ground contact ; One ground pass per orbit,
lasting 8 minutes– Assume 50% of passes occur in sunlight, 50% in eclipse

## Special ADCS mode / momentum dump

+10 W for 5 minutes per orbit, in sunlight

## Baseline Data Budget

Data Flow Using the baseline case (10 minutes imaging per orbit, 2 Gbit generated per
orbit, downlink 50 Mbps for 8 minutes)
